QuickGestures (2026)

A React web app designed for artists to practice gesture and figure drawing with timed sessions.

Choose from quick presets like warm-ups, standard practice, or long poses, or build custom sequences with multiple timed segments. Manage your own reference image library with tagging, filtering, and folder uploads. Save session presets for quick access, switch between Sequence and Infinite modes, and browse a curated collection of art resources and tutorials.
